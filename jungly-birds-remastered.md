# Privacy Policy — Jungly Birds Remastered

**Last updated:** March 20, 2026

Jungly Birds Remastered is developed by RoiSoftStudio.

## Data Collection

This app does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or information of any kind.

## Local Storage

The only data stored is your high score and play count, which are saved locally on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.

## Third-Party Services

This app uses **Google Play Games Services** for leaderboards and achievements. When you sign in to Play Games, Google may collect data according to their own privacy policy. This is managed entirely by Google — we do not access or store any of your Google account information.

For details on how Google handles your data, see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Ads and Analytics

This app contains no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

## Children's Privacy

This app does not collect any data from any user, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
