# Privacy Policy — Gravity Flip Runner

**Last updated:** February 17, 2026

Gravity Flip Runner is developed by RoiSoftStudio.

## Data Collection

This app does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or information of any kind. The app runs entirely offline on your device.

## Local Storage

The only data stored is your high score, which is saved locally on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

## Third-Party Services

This app contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party services.

## Children's Privacy

This app does not collect any data from any user, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
